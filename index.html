YEAR 2089 CE

The Transition changed everything — but it didn't settle the old arguments. It just made them stranger, faster, and higher-stakes. Substrate rights. Post-scarcity distribution. The right to remain unenhanced. Planetary governance vs. nation-state memory.

These are the tensions your generation will actually decide. Not abstractions — real forks in civilisational history. There are no correct answers here. There's only where you land.
